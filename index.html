Why This Matters

Bitcoin's roadmap emerges from thousands of distributed technical decisions. This network view helps researchers, builders, and capital allocators separate durable engineering signal from short-lived hype.

By mapping code, review, standards, and discussion into one contributor graph, Orange Dev Network surfaces who is actively shaping consensus and where protocol attention is moving next.
