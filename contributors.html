The Contributor Galaxy

Visual history of every developer. Each bubble represents a human story. (White Glow = Contributed in last year)

Y: Commits (Log)
X: Joined Year

Reading the Galaxy

Notice the "Floor" of the galaxy: Hundreds of developers contribute once or twice and then fade away—the decentralized periphery. The "Stars" at the top are the Core Maintainers and long-tenured veterans who have dedicated years to the protocol. The Y-axis is logarithmic to ensure that a developer with 10 commits and one with 10,000 are both visible on the same canvas.
