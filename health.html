Workforce Stability: Tracks the stability of the active project team. It focuses on "Regulars" (contributors with 3+ commits in a given year), regardless of when they first joined, and shows how many remain active in subsequent years. This measures system continuity.

New Joiner Loyalty: Tracks the survival of arrival cohorts. It specifically filters for new developers who joined in that year AND demonstrated immediate commitment (3+ commits in their first year). This measures onboarding health by excluding casual one-time contributors.
